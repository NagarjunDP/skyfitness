KNOW YOUR NUMBERS.

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a foundational indicator of body composition and mass distribution. Calculate your score to align your fitness targets at Sky Fitness Gym.

STANDARD ADULT BMI CATEGORIES:

  • Below 18.5: Underweight
  • 18.5 – 24.9: Normal Weight (Optimal Target)
  • 25.0 – 29.9: Overweight
  • 30.0+: Obesity
